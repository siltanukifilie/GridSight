# GridSight Team

**Developed by the GridSight Team.**

GridSight brings together product design, Python engineering, data analysis and
mini-grid planning workflows for developers and investors/lenders.

Individual member names are not published here until the team supplies its
preferred credits. Git commit authors record contributions, not a complete team
roster or a statement of ownership of third-party data.

## Acknowledgements

Public-data providers, researchers and open-source maintainers retain credit
for their work. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the
[data catalogue](data/README.md).

AI tools assisted implementation, testing and documentation. The
[disclosure](docs/DISCLOSURE.md) distinguishes that assistance from team review,
data provenance and evidence still needed.
