GRIDSIGHT MODEL COMPARISON — RESULTS

Eight approaches, trained/evaluated separately at three operating sites.
Models were chosen using validation MAE, before test scoring.

FROZEN VALIDATION CHOICES, SCORED ON LATER TEST HOURS
Site             Selected approach          Test hours    MAE kW    WAPE
Rwanda ECOS      Ridge regression                 64       0.0395    1.25%
Congo Idjwi      Last-hour persistence             86       0.1998    6.67%
Haiti Beaumont   Extra Trees                      110       0.0401   28.31%

MAE is mean absolute error, in kW. WAPE is total absolute prediction error divided
by total absolute observed power. It is not '100 minus accuracy'. Lower is better.
WAPE reflects the total level and errors during the scored test hours, not every
hour of the site campaign. Haiti's low mean load makes relative error sizable.

INTERPRETATION
Rwanda: Ridge reduces test MAE by 3.93% versus the last-hour baseline. This is a
modest result on only 64 test hours; no significance or deployment claim is made.
Congo: none of the five ML families beats last-hour persistence in validation.
Persistence also outperforms every tested ML family on this test period. Keep
the baseline as the exploratory choice instead of forcing a complex model.
Haiti: Extra Trees reduces test MAE by 25.79% versus persistence, but test WAPE
is still 28.31%, so the remaining relative error must stay visible.

All approaches used the same site-specific rows. No test-based reselection or
retraining took place. The model choices happened also to have the lowest test MAE
in these runs, but they remain choices made on validation, not on test rankings.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
No prediction for an unbuilt Bugesera mini-grid, no validated monthly kWh forecast,
no learned annual growth and no calibrated uncertainty interval. This is later-hour
testing at already-observed operating sites, NOT validation against held-out sites.
The campaigns were previously inspected, so this is exploratory evidence.
