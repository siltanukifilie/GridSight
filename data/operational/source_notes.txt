GRIDSIGHT — KIT THREE-SITE MEASURED-POWER REFERENCE
Prepared 1 November 2026. Separate supplement, not a replacement for Bugesera.

CONFIRMED AND INCLUDED
Three complete published measurement CSVs and the original units/notes workbook:
Rwanda ECOS demand: 24,856 rows, 21 November–9 December 2021.
DR Congo Idjwi main distribution: 399,644 rows, 16 January–21 February 2022.
Haiti Beaumont: 44,928 rows, 25 November–26 December 2021.
Total: 469,428 source rows across three distinct microgrids.
Dates above are UTC. These are short campaigns, NOT multi-year histories.
Raw source files remain unchanged. No synthetic measurements are included.

START
Open GridSight_KIT_Summary.xlsx for the site overview and hourly sample summaries.
processed/site_catalog.csv gives source coverage and quality diagnostics.
processed/*_power.csv preserves source order and selected measurements.
processed/hourly_sample_summaries.csv groups valid timestamps into UTC hours.
raw/ contains the three complete original CSV files and source dictionary.
metadata/ contains definitions, checks, hashes and proposed site-level folds.

MEANING OF THE MEASUREMENTS
The source dictionary defines P as mean active power in watts. It is not a
consumer monthly kWh bill. Rwanda power is P_1+P_2+P_3, Congo is P_tot, Haiti P_1.
Rwanda's branches are connected to one generator phase, per publisher notes.
No voltage-current shortcut or guessed power factor is used.
No monthly kWh, growth rate, uncertainty bounds or demand forecasts are invented.

PROCESSING AND QUALITY
Original timestamps parsed with DD.MM.YYYY HH:MM:SS. UTC values checked against
Unix timestamps. Missing numeric readings are not filled with zero.
Congo has 24 extra duplicate timestamps (48 rows in affected groups) and one
backward timestamp step. All source rows are retained; all 48 ambiguous rows
are excluded from hourly summaries. Other sites have no duplicate timestamps.
Hourly summaries are arithmetic sample means/minima/maxima, NOT time-weighted
energy. Missing hours are absent, not zero. Partial hours remain in the table.
Sample count ratio is valid samples divided by 3600/median spacing; it is only
a sample-count diagnostic, not validated time coverage or an energy correction.
Site-level means/maxima describe all published rows, including duplicate rows.
No clipping, smoothing, outlier removal or additional imputation was performed.
Maximum gaps: about 11.5 hours Rwanda, 95.3 hours Congo, 48.4 minutes Haiti.
The publisher may have preprocessed measurements; these are not claimed to be
unmodified sensor telemetry. See the original source notes for excluded channels.

CRITICAL OPERATING CONDITIONS
Rwanda: damaged generator windings and broken electronic load controller.
Congo: broken load controller, voltage/frequency limitations, diesel switching.
Haiti: nominal PV inverter voltage changed on 29 November.
Measured supplied power under these conditions need not equal unconstrained
electricity demand. Do not silently interpret low power as low consumer need.

HELD-OUT SITE VALIDATION
The fold CSV proposes three leave-one-site-out partitions: one entire site for
testing and the other two for training. It is a protocol, NOT evaluation results.
Never split hourly rows at random and claim testing on unseen sites.
Sites differ in country, technology, served loads and operating conditions.
Three sites are inadequate for robust validation of a 20-feature model; matched
pre-connection household and business features are not supplied here. This pack
supports data-pipeline checks and exploratory transfer experiments only.
The short records cannot validate first-years growth or annual growth rates.
Rwanda ECOS is NOT Bugesera, and Haiti is NOT East Africa. Do not join outcomes
to Bugesera sectors or treat foreign-site tests as Rwanda-wide validation.
No model was trained or evaluated in preparing this dataset.

REPRODUCTION
With Python plus pandas/numpy/openpyxl installed, run: python prepare.py
This rebuilds processed CSVs and metadata from the included raw files.
The source serves a TAR stream (not a ZIP) at:
https://radar.kit.edu/radar/de/dataset/PxzzPHZdpMnlkMDH/datasetBinaryTreeStream
To refresh, download to KIT.tar and run: python prepare.py --archive KIT.tar
The collector selected only the three primary measurement files and dictionary;
the complete repository contains additional generation/branch/device files.
Selected file lengths were checked against their TAR entry sizes. Per-file
SHA-256 hashes identify the local snapshot. These are not publisher-certified
checksums; the repository's BagIt archive MD5 does not apply to a TAR stream.
The partial/full transport archive is not part of this selected-file release.
Workbook rebuilding requires Node and @oai/artifact-tool: node build_workbook.mjs
Run python package.py after rebuilding to create the distribution ZIP.
