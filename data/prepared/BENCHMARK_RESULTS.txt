GRIDSIGHT — WHAT THIS EVALUATION TELLS US
1 November 2026

BOTTOM LINE
We completed an exploratory whole-site holdout test on real published electrical
power measurements. The two simple baselines do not transfer reliably across
these very different systems. This is useful evidence of a limitation, not proof
that a Bugesera pre-construction demand forecaster is ready.

Measured data source: KIT, https://doi.org/10.35097/1305, CC BY 4.0.
No synthetic measurements were added. Predictions are separate model outputs.

PRIMARY BASELINE: EQUAL-SITE LOCAL-HOUR PROFILE
At each local hour, predict the average of the other two sites' hour profiles.
The target is hourly sample-mean active power in kW, NOT monthly energy in kWh.

Held-out site       Scored hours   Actual mean   MAE     RMSE    Bias     WAPE
Rwanda / ECOS       412            3.088 kW      1.243   1.293  -1.243     40.3%
DR Congo / Idjwi    463            3.508 kW      2.047   2.630  -1.903     58.3%
Haiti / Beaumont    745            0.127 kW      3.192   3.210  +3.192   2503.9%

MAE, RMSE and bias are in kW. WAPE is an error measure, not model accuracy;
values above 100% are possible. No "100 minus WAPE" accuracy claim is valid.
The Haiti baseline predicts 3.320 kW on average for measured mean 0.127 kW.
The mismatch in site scale is substantial; extra hours do not solve this.

COMPARISON BASELINE: CONSTANT FROM THE OTHER TWO SITE MEANS
MAE: Rwanda 1.270 kW; DR Congo 2.045 kW; Haiti 3.171 kW.
The local-hour model slightly improves Rwanda MAE but not the other two sites.
Rwanda RMSE also increases, so even that improvement is metric-dependent.
These comparisons are descriptive, not statistically significant model rankings.
Both baselines were fixed before the first scoring run; neither was tuned to
improve the held-out errors. Fold training sets overlap, and hourly observations
are correlated. The folds and hours must not be counted as independent trials.

DATA QUALITY
1,620 of 2,035 calendar hours are scored. The other 415 are excluded, not zero.
Rwanda: 412/427 hours (96.5%). Congo: 463/856 (54.1%). Haiti: 745/752 (99.1%).
48 rows belonging to duplicate Congo timestamp groups were removed.
Scores describe retained hours only. Large Congo gaps limit representativeness.
Source notes document equipment problems and different operating conditions;
measured delivered power need not equal unconstrained customer demand.

WHAT WE CAN CLAIM TO THE JUDGES
"We implemented a leakage-controlled, leave-one-site-out evaluation on three
published microgrid measurement campaigns. Baseline transfer errors were large,
demonstrating the need for comparable sites and matched pre-connection features.
Validation of Bugesera monthly demand and multi-year growth remains pending."

WHAT WE CANNOT CLAIM
- A validated 20-feature model or an accurate capacity-sizing recommendation.
- Validated Bugesera monthly electricity demand, growth or uncertainty bounds.
- East-Africa-only evaluation: this reference set includes Rwanda, Congo and Haiti.
- Unconstrained demand or billed energy measured by these short campaigns.
- Access to Renewvia data or any operator data not actually received.

NEXT DATA GATE
Obtain multiple comparable sites with de-identified site IDs, consumption units,
timestamp/timezone, commissioning dates, connection counts and missing/outage
flags, matched to pre-connection household, business, appliance and tariff data.
Growth validation needs records covering the intended post-connection years.
Keep complete sites out of training, tune only within training sites, and assess
monthly errors plus interval coverage separately. No universal minimum site count
is claimed here; three heterogeneous sites cannot establish broad generalization.
