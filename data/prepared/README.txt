GridSight prepared evidence snapshot — 1 November 2026

This compact pack contains Bugesera sector context, source/feature summaries,
preparation status and a separate published measured-power benchmark. It does
not contain matched Bugesera consumption labels or a new trained model.
The live engineering calculation is unchanged. Low / expected / high and
36-month growth remain scenarios, not calibrated intervals or observed growth.

SCOPES AND MISSING VALUES
All 15 Bugesera rows describe whole administrative sectors, NOT cells or proposed
service areas. Never copy their household totals into a small project catchment.
Blank CSV values / JSON null mean unavailable, not zero. Census: 2022. Boundaries:
2012 representation. Buildings: Google 2023 release. OSM: 2026-10-31 snapshot,
incomplete and potentially duplicated. GridFinder: 2019 study / 2020 archive,
not current utility verification. Nightlights are indirect GEP inputs with exact
VIIRS vintage unresolved, not a newly downloaded NOAA raster. NASA POWER is
coarse gridded context, not a local station. Non-grid does not mean unelectrified.
Estimated phone/TV-owning households are NOT numbers of devices.

BENCHMARK AND PREPARATION
KIT Rwanda ECOS is not Bugesera. Haiti is outside East Africa. Campaigns cover
18–36 days in 2021–2022, with equipment faults and gaps. Target: arithmetic
sample-mean delivered active power, kW, not integrated/billed monthly kWh or
unconstrained demand. Both exploratory whole-site holdout baselines are supplied.
WAPE is an error measure and can exceed 100%; do not report 100 minus WAPE as
accuracy. Results demonstrate poor transfer, not current-model validation.
The 1,540 prepared operational examples use calendar/past-power predictors,
not the original 20 pre-construction inputs. Splits and training-only processing
are separate per site. Preparation trained no prediction model. No multi-year
growth labels exist. Training matrices and raw telemetry are not duplicated here.
BENCHMARK_METHOD/RESULTS preserve upstream methodology; their references to
plots, scripts and raw inputs describe the original pack, not this summary ZIP.
