# Development and evidence disclosure

Developed by the **GridSight Team** for the Global Energy Hackathon mini-grid
demand challenge. AI tools, including Codex, assisted with implementation,
debugging, tests, data-integration code and documentation. This assistance is
not a claim of independent scientific review or operator approval.

## Data and models

- Public context and public measured studies retain their source attribution and
  reuse terms. The original authors/operators do not endorse GridSight.
- The default planning equation and its growth/cost assumptions are separate
  from the frozen synthetic model and separately reported measured experiments.
- Low / expected / high are scenarios. No new Rwanda pre-construction accuracy,
  calibrated three-year confidence or realized capital-saving claim is made.
- The public repository omits confidential operator files, customer identifiers,
  restricted survey/utility layers and local user export bundles.
